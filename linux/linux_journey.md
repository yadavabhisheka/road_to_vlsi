#### Getting Started
- **Linux History**
![Linux History](resources/linux_history.webp)

- **The Role of Kernel** - It acts as a bridge, allowing the hardware to communicate with the software. The kernel manages system resources, such as the CPU, memory, and peripheral devices.
  
- **Choosing a Linux Distribution** - Kernel is just a part of these distros. The complete operating systems built around the Linux kernel are called **Linux distributions**, or **Linux distros**.

- **Debian** - Debian follows a release-based model. The project periodically publishes a new Stable release after packages have matured through development and testing. Package management is one of Debian's biggest strengths. Debian uses the .deb package format and the APT toolset to install, update, remove, and manage software.

- **RHEL** - RHEL is important because it gives organizations a stable and supported platform for production workloads. RHEL uses the RPM package format and tools such as DNF to install, update, and manage software. One of the biggest reasons organizations choose RHEL is enterprise support. RHEL is also closely associated with professional training and certification. Credentials such as RHCSA and RHCE are well known in Linux administration and are part of why RHEL remains highly visible in professional environments.

- **Ubuntu** - Ubuntu is popular because it tries to make Linux practical for everyday use. It offers a polished installer, strong documentation, predictable releases, and a large ecosystem of tutorials and third-party support. Ubuntu is a Debian-based distribution, which means it inherits much of its package management model and software packaging approach from Debian.Even i am using it from past 2 years as my main desktop os.

- **Fedora** - Fedora plays an important role in the Red Hat ecosystem. New technologies and changes often appear in Fedora first, and some of that work later influences Red Hat Enterprise Linux. This relationship helps explain why Fedora feels more current while RHEL is more conservative and enterprise-focused.
